## 1. Realm Overview

The Sky Citadel is not a single tower or fortress, but a **cluster of floating structures** tethered to each other by bridges of woven cloud-silk and bound by ancient wind-current rituals. During the First Harmony, it was a **realm of boundless discovery** — a place where Astra Pathfinders trained, where the edge of the known world was not a barrier but a beginning, and where the night sky was mapped with such precision that citizens could predict celestial events centuries in advance.

Now, the Sky Citadel **drifts in incomplete currents**. Some spires remain connected; others orbit in lonely loops, unreachable. The great Observatory Dome — once open to the entire sky — is partially shuttered, its mirrors dulled by age and isolation. The **routes between spires** have become unstable, and the **Pathfinder Guild Hall** stands empty except for archived maps that no one can read anymore.

**Canonical Anchors:**  
- Realm Theme: Discovery  
- Wound of Discovery = Isolation = routes broke, knowledge fragmented  
- Skykin companion family native to this Realm

## 2. Cultural Details

### Architecture
- **Floating Spires:** Residential, Observatory, Archive Annex, Guild Hall, Wind Garden  
- **Cloud-Silk Bridges:** Bio-luminescent walkways that pulse with safe paths  
- **Wind-Cisterns:** Structures that capture and channel wind for transport/energy  
- **Celestial Markers:** Spires topped with constellation-aligned spires or orbs  

### Traditions
- **Starwalking Festival:** Citizens and Companions "walk" the safe wind-currents to visit distant spires  
- **Chartweaving:** Collaborative mapping where each citizen contributes one route or star pattern  
- **Pathfinder’s Oath:** Ceremonial naming of new discovery routes, tied to Companion witnessing  

### Food & Music
- **Food:** Light, airy — cloud-bread, mist-tea, fruit from sky-gardens  
- **Music:** Aeolian harps, wind-chimes, vocal harmonies that mimic wind sounds  

### Governance
- **Windward Council:** 7 Pathfinders + 1 Skykin Companion = decision-making body  
- **Guild of Currentriders:** Maintains bridges, teaches navigation  
- **Observatory Collective:** Records celestial events, predicts safe travel windows  

**Canonical Anchors:**  
- Skykin companion family (Discovery, Exploration, Freedom)  
- Astra House (Discovery Pillar)  
- Pathfinder’s Mark puzzle type

## 3. Connection to the Wound of Discovery

The Sky Citadel’s isolation is not physical but **relational**. Its spires are still there; its maps still exist. But the **shared knowledge of how to move between them** has fragmented. Citizens once spoke the names of wind-currents like prayers — *Zephyr’s Grace, Norther’s Swiftness, Stormtamer’s Hold* — but now those names are half-forgotten.

The **Pathfinder’s Mark** puzzles scattered throughout the Realm are not simple route-tracing exercises. They are **archaeological fragments** of the old navigation system — incomplete glyphs, faded constellation maps, partial wind-song sequences that, when restored, slowly re-stabilize a single current or unlock one shuttered Observatory chamber.

**Canonical Anchors:**  
- Wound = Fragmentation of shared knowledge, not destruction  
- Restoration = Reconnection + Rediscovery  
- Pathfinder’s Mark puzzle type (Tier 1–4 alignment)

- **Old Gyre (retired Skykin):** Lives in the Observatory Dome, refuses to fly but remembers every star

- Relationships are part of restoration — not just mechanics

## 6. Restoration Goals (Guardian Actions)

1. **Realign Observatory Mirror** (Harmony Alignment puzzle)  
   - Restores ability to see distant Realm signals  
   - Unlocks new tier of Archive Fragments about cross-Realm contact  

2. **Decipher Aelion’s Cipher** (Fragment Assembly + Echo Sequence)  
   - Combines Aelion’s fragmented notes with a Companion memory of last full festival  
   - Reveals the "Lost Current" that once connected to the Celestial Nexus  

3. **Restore Safe Wind-Currents** (Pathfinder’s Mark × 3)  
   - Stabilize three key bridges between major spires  
   - Unlock Guild Hall, Festival Grounds, and Wind Garden  

4. **Revive Starwalking Festival** (Community Pattern puzzle)  
   - Requires coordination with citizens and Companions  
   - Restores social/cultural function of the Realm  

5. **Bond with Nimbus** (Companion Relationship quest arc)  
   - Not a puzzle — narrative choice builds trust  
   - Unlocks Nimbus-specific Echo Sequence and Pathfinder’s Mark enhancements
