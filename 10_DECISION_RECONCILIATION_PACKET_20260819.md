## Autonomous Handling Rule

While no creator decision is supplied, autonomous work will: preserve all options; mark derivative work `Draft` or `Proposed`; avoid exact unresolved lore claims; avoid scope increases; use the documented no-combat/restorative baseline; and continue with non-dependent inventories, cross-reference cleanup, implementation-readiness preparation, and accessibility-oriented review.
