## CONFLICT 1 — Book of Harmony: Living Document vs. Fixed Original Text

**Conflict**
- Vol 1 Transfer (ChatGPT export): *"The Book of Harmony is a living document. New entries could be added. Old entries were preserved. Contradictions were often retained rather than removed."*
- Perplexity session canon (confirmed by user): *"Original text is fixed. Future generations add commentary and interpretation. Volume VIII (On Belonging) is the hidden volume written by ordinary citizens."*

**Sources in conflict**
- ChatGPT Vol 1 export (older, lower authority)
- Perplexity session explicit user statement (highest authority)

**Resolution: APPLY — Perplexity canon wins**
The original text of the Book of Harmony is **fixed**. It cannot be altered. What grows over time is the body of **commentary, reflection, and interpretation** that accumulates around it — much like the Talmud model, where the original stands untouched and centuries of response surround it. Volume VIII (On Belonging) is the one exceptional volume: it was not written by a single author but compiled from the words of ordinary citizens across generations, and is itself considered part of the fixed canon rather than commentary.

**Practical implication for Tier 1 development**
When writing the Book of Harmony, the original seven volumes must be written as if they are ancient, authoritative, and complete. Commentary passages from later eras can be added as clearly labeled secondary text. Volume VIII should be written as a mosaic of short citizen voices — not a single authored chapter.

## CONFLICT 2 — "Hall of Guardians" vs. "Book of Guardians"

**Conflict**
- Vol 2 Transfer (Realms section): Legacy Realm contains a "**Hall of Guardians**"
- Confirmed Great Archive internal sections: one of the 7 sections is the "**Book of Guardians**"

**Sources in conflict**
- ChatGPT Vol 2 export (Realms section)
- Perplexity continuity summary (Archive section — higher authority)

**Resolution: APPLY — These are two distinct things**
These are not duplicates. They serve different purposes and exist in different locations:
- **Book of Guardians** (inside the Great Archive, Arena Realm): a written record of Guardian contributions — names, deeds, legacies. A living chronicle.
- **Hall of Guardians** (inside the Legacy Realm): a physical commemorative space — architecture, installations, tributes. The embodied memory of extraordinary contributors.

The Archive records. The Legacy Realm honors. Both can coexist without conflict.

**Practical implication**
When developing the Great Archive (Tier 4) and the Legacy Realm (Tier 2), treat these as complementary institutions with a deliberate relationship: the Archive creates the record; the Legacy Realm makes it visible and experiential.

## CONFLICT 11 — First Harmony Duration / Threshold Model

**Conflict**
- Draft `05-first-harmony.md` specifies 3–4 century duration with gradual Threshold model
- `09-restoration-era.md` and `04-book-of-harmony.md` reference First Harmony span
- Asset Registry Flag #14 and STATUS-FINAL-REPORT blocked item #9: duration + Threshold model pending creator confirmation
- Canon constraint: No sudden catastrophe/shattering event as antagonist act (Rules 2.1, 2.2)

**Sources in conflict**
- Tier 1 draft documents (working draft canon)
- Asset Registry / Status Report (flagging need for explicit lock)

**Resolution: APPLY — Option A: 3–4 Centuries with Gradual Generational Drift Threshold**
**Decision Date:** 2026-08-04
**Authority:** Creator confirmation — Status Mode session

**Locked Canon:**
- **Duration:** 3–4 centuries (approximately 300–400 years)
- **Threshold Model:** Gradual generational drift — not a sudden event

**Threshold Mechanics (Canon):**
1. **Pillar Decoupling** — The Seven Pillars (Knowledge, Discovery, Growth, Memory, Resilience, Harmony, Belonging) slowly lost their systemic interconnection across generations
2. **Crystal Network Latency** — Node resonance synchronization degraded incrementally; knowledge fragments arrived late, then out of order, then not at all
3. **Lantern Cycle Desynchronization** — The Lantern Network's shared timing (cross-Realm coordination pulses) drifted out of phase; Lanterns no longer lit in harmony
4. **Festival Participation Decline** — Cross-Realm festival attendance dwindled generation by generation; communities stopped sending representatives; traditions became local-only
5. **Pathfinder Route Atrophy** — Astra Pathfinders found fewer maintained waypoints each season; routes closed not from disaster but from disuse
6. **Companion Memory Gaps** — Companions began holding memories that no longer had living context; the Archive received fragments without connective tissue

**The Shattering** = The cumulative consequence of unaddressed Threshold drift:
- Not an event. A recognition moment when civilization realized its systems had silently failed.
- No antagonist. No "darkness spread." No "Crystal Heart shattered."
- The Shattering is the name later generations gave to the **outcome** of the Threshold.

**Restoration Era Begins** when:
- First Guardians recognize the drift and choose to act
- First Lantern is relit by deliberate care (not automatic)
- First Crystal Network node is manually recalibrated
- First cross-Realm festival invitation is sent and answered

**Downstream Canon Locks:**
- `05-first-harmony.md` — Duration and Threshold model locked
- `09-restoration-era.md` — Restoration Era start defined relative to Threshold recognition
- `04-book-of-harmony.md` — Principles reference 3–4 century First Harmony span
- All Realm packets — Ancient sites dated "Late First Harmony (c. Year 320)" etc.
- Companion packets — Elder companions (Echo, Frostpaw) memories span late First Harmony
- IMP-02 Five Wounds Restoration Model — Wounds originated in Threshold drift, not Shattering event

**Status: RESOLVED — Option A locked as canon**

**Sources in conflict**

**Decision Date:** 2026-08-04
**Authority:** Creator confirmation — Status Mode session

All power-ups, boosters, special gems, and combo systems are **reframed as puzzle tools and restorative aids** — not power advantages. They pass the Puzzle Design Test independently.

| Sparkle | **Glimmer Hint** | Hearthlight tradition: a gentle glimmer shows where attention is needed | Draw awareness to a community pattern gap | Subtly indicates one Community Pattern contribution point |
| Crystal Pearl | **Archive Lens** | Borealis Chroniclers crafted lenses to read fragmented Archive records | Decode partial fragments without assembly | Reveals one Fragment Assembly piece's correct orientation |
| Moonlight Blast | **Twilight Veil** | Legacy House used twilight veils to protect sealed legacies from premature opening | Guard Legacy Seals until Guardian demonstrates readiness | Temporarily shows Legacy Seal structure without solving it |

### Special Gems → Puzzle Variants
| Original | Canon-Reframed | Civilizational Purpose | Puzzle Function |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bomb Gem | **Resonance Gem** | Crystal Network self-repair element: when activated, resonates across adjacent nodes | In Crystal Resonance: reveals adjacent pattern fragments (3×3 area) |
| Lightning Gem | **Pathfinder's Beacon** | Astra emergency beacon: illuminates connected waypoints along a route | In Pathfinder's Mark: reveals connected route segment |

|---|---|---|---|

| 4x+ multiplier | **Harmony Convergence** | Multiple puzzle elements align; the civilization responds to coherent care | Brief harmonic convergence visual — restoration moment |

**Key Principles Applied:**
1. **No destruction** — tools reveal, illuminate, guide, resonate; they never "blast," "destroy," or "clear"
2. **Civilizational origin** — every tool has a maker, a purpose, a history in the First Harmony
3. **Puzzle Design Test** — each tool answers: Why does it exist? Who made it? What was its purpose? What does it reveal? What does it restore?
4. **Restoration language** — "reveals," "illuminates," "guides," "resonates," "attunes" — not "destroys," "explodes," "multiplies," "powers up"
5. **Visual feedback only** — Harmony Flow provides acknowledgment (glow, chime, convergence); no score multiplication, no resource bonus, no gameplay advantage
6. **Accessibility-segregated** — if assist tools exist, they are explicitly labeled "Guidance Options" and do not affect Archive reveals or progression
