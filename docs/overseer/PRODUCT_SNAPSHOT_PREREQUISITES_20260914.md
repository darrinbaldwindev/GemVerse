# GemVerse Product Snapshot Prerequisites — 2026-09-14 AEST

**Purpose:** Define the minimum authoritative product snapshot required before Marketing or other portfolio agents make concrete commercial, launch, or capability claims about GemVerse.

## Minimum snapshot fields

1. **Product boundary** — what GemVerse is at the current stage: game prototype, playable vertical slice, design/IP package, or another explicitly approved state.
2. **Primary audience** — intended player/customer segment and age range, if approved.
3. **First usable experience** — what a new user can actually do today, verified from runnable evidence.
4. **Platform state** — browser/mobile/desktop/Construct status, separated into verified vs proposed.
5. **Arena MVP scope** — included interactions, puzzle types, narrative beats, onboarding, companion presence, persistence, and offline behavior only where verified.
6. **Monetization status** — approved, proposed, or unknown. Do not infer pricing, purchases, subscriptions, ads, gacha, or premium mechanics.
7. **Brand/canon baseline** — approved tagline, tone, exclusions, and currently active realm/character terminology.
8. **Implementation readiness** — source availability, reproducible build/run state, tests, known blockers.
9. **Launch status** — internal prototype / pre-alpha / alpha / beta / released only if explicitly evidenced and approved.
10. **Evidence links** — immutable repo refs, build/test receipts, and creator-approved decision records supporting each claim.

## Current safe marketing boundary

Marketing may safely describe the verified world/brand direction and the project’s restorative puzzle-fantasy intent. It must not currently claim that the Arena is playable, production-ready, released, cross-platform, monetized, or technically complete unless those claims are backed by recovered and reproducible implementation evidence.

## Unblock condition

Marketing may move from discovery/positioning to concrete product claims once the authoritative product snapshot is populated from verified current evidence and owner-approved commercial decisions.
