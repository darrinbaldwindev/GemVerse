# GemVerse Tier 1 Readiness Report
**Version:** 1.0
**Purpose:** For each Tier 1 focus area, summarize what is safe to build on and list the key questions that must be answered before production writing begins.
**Tier 1 Order:** Book of Harmony → First Harmony → Five Wounds → Crystal Network → Lantern Network → Restoration Era

---

## OVERVIEW — What "Ready" Means

A Tier 1 entry is ready for production writing when:
1. The philosophical foundation is confirmed and has no unresolved conflicts
2. The structural framework (what sections it must contain) is agreed upon
3. There are no upstream dependencies that could force a rewrite

All six Tier 1 areas meet criteria 1 and 2. Criteria 3 has one dependency: **the Book of Harmony must be written first**, because it is referenced by the First Harmony, the Five Wounds, and the Restoration Era. The correct order is the confirmed order.

---

## 1. BOOK OF HARMONY

### What is confirmed and safe to build on

**Identity (fully locked)**
- It is the foundational wisdom text of the First Harmony — not a religion, prophecy, or legal code
- It is a collection of observations, stories, lessons, reflections, discoveries, and principles
- Archive scholars describe it as: *"The memory of how civilization learned to live together."*
- Tone: hopeful, reflective, curious, humble, non-dogmatic
- Original text is fixed (Conflict 1 resolved); commentary tradition grows around it

**Structure (locked)**
Eight volumes with section types including:
- Reflections, Observations, Parables, Historical Accounts
- Guardian Contributions, Pillar Teachings, Questions Without Answers, Companion Testimonies
- Volume VIII (On Belonging) — hidden volume, compiled from ordinary citizens' words, not single-authored

**Thematic principles (12 — draft status, ready for refinement)**
- Balance requires effort
- Knowledge without wisdom creates imbalance
- Discovery without responsibility creates imbalance
- Growth without memory creates imbalance
- Memory without growth creates stagnation
- Harmony emerges through contribution
- Belonging cannot be commanded — it must be cultivated
- Civilization survives when memory survives
- No individual holds the whole truth
- Wisdom emerges collectively
- (Two more implied: Community requires participation; Legacy requires intention)

**Known example fragments (4 thematic fragments — draft status)**
1. *"To remember alone is preservation. To remember together is civilization."*
2. *"Every lantern lights a path. Together they illuminate a world."*
3. *"What is discovered belongs first to wonder, then to understanding, then to those who come after."*
4. *"The future is not inherited. It is entrusted."*

### Key questions to answer before writing

1. **Who wrote the original text?** Was it one author, a council, a collective? Were the authors Harmonists? Members of the Harmony Council? This determines the voice and authority of the text.
2. **When was it written?** Early First Harmony, late First Harmony, or compiled across the entire era?
3. **How was it organized?** Did the eight volumes correspond to the seven Pillars + Belonging, or were they organized differently?
4. **What survived the Shattering?** Is the version Guardians encounter complete, fragmentary, or partially reconstructed?
5. **Who adds commentary?** Is commentary added by the Archive? By Scholars on the Scholar Path? By Guardians themselves?
6. **What language/voice register?** Ancient and formal? Accessible and warm? Different volumes in different voices?

### Writing brief

The Book of Harmony entry must produce:
- [ ] Header entry (what it is, where it exists, who created it)
- [ ] Eight volume titles and one-paragraph descriptions
- [ ] 12 finalized principles (polished, quotable)
- [ ] 8–12 finalized fragment passages (from different volumes and voices)
- [ ] At least 3 fragments specifically from Volume VIII (citizen voices)
- [ ] Commentary tradition description (how it works, who participates)
- [ ] Note on what survived the Shattering and what is still lost

## 2. FIRST HARMONY

### What is confirmed and safe to build on

**Era identity (fully locked)**
- Greatest age of civilization before the Shattering
- Balanced, not perfect — important distinction
- All Seven Pillars in healthy relationship; Belonging emerged naturally
- Did not collapse suddenly — slow Pillar drift led to the Shattering

**Major institutions (all confirmed)**
Great Archive, seven Houses, six Paths, Crystal Network, Lantern Network, Harmony Council, Book of Harmony

**Daily life philosophy (confirmed)**
- Citizens contributed according to strengths
- Discovery was celebrated; learning was accessible
- Companions participated in society
- Festivals connected communities
- Memory preservation was considered civic work
- Mentorship was highly respected
- Knowledge, stories, and people moved freely across the world

**Early signs of imbalance (confirmed)**
- Pillars slowly drifted apart
- Knowledge disconnected from wisdom
- Discovery disconnected from responsibility
- Growth disconnected from memory
- Communities became increasingly specialized
- Civilization gradually lost sense of interconnectedness

### Key questions to answer before writing

1. **How long did the First Harmony last?** Was it centuries? Millennia? The duration affects how it is described (a golden age vs. a brief window of balance).
2. **What did governance look like?** Beyond the Harmony Council, who led? Was there a single civilization government or a federation of Realms?
3. **How did education work?** Was the University of Harmony already active? Were the Paths a formal system or informal traditions?
4. **What was a typical citizen's life?** What did someone do in a day? What did home look like? What did they eat? (This feeds directly into Tier 2 Realm Cultures.)
5. **How did Companions participate?** Were they citizens? Did they have specific roles? Were they recognized formally?
6. **What triggered the drift?** No villain — but what was the proximate cause? Was it success creating complacency? Population growth creating specialization? A philosophical shift?

### Writing brief

The First Harmony entry must produce:
- [ ] Era overview (what it was, how it felt, why it mattered)
- [ ] Timeline sketch (when it began, how long it lasted, when it ended)
- [ ] Description of each major institution during this era
- [ ] Daily life narrative (1–2 paragraphs from the perspective of an ordinary citizen)
- [ ] Governance structure
- [ ] How Belonging emerged naturally (with specific examples)
- [ ] The slow drift — what changed and in what order
- [ ] Connection to the Shattering (how the First Harmony ended)

## 3. THE FIVE WOUNDS

### What is confirmed and safe to build on

**Identity (locked)**
- Five civilization-scale fractures caused by the Shattering
- Not physical, not villain-caused — they represent what civilization lost
- Each Wound corresponds to Pillar disconnection

**Official names and themes (locked canon — creator confirmation, 2026-08-19)**
1. Wound of Knowledge — Fragmentation (knowledge stopped flowing)
2. Wound of Discovery — Isolation (realms drifted apart)
3. Wound of Growth — Decay (communities stopped nurturing the future)
4. Wound of Memory — Forgetting (history became uncertain)
5. Wound of Belonging — Division (citizens stopped feeling connected) — likely the deepest

**Confirmed consequences (apply to all)**
Broken connections, lost memories, fragmented knowledge, isolated communities, unanswered questions, lost traditions, interrupted legacy

**Current status in the game world**
Multiple Wounds are still active during the Restoration Era. Guardian actions address them. No official severity ranking.

### Key questions to answer before writing

1. **Did the Wounds appear simultaneously or in sequence?** If sequentially, what order? Which came first?
2. **Are there visible physical manifestations?** For example: does the Wound of Memory cause Crystal Network nodes to go dark? Does the Wound of Belonging cause Lantern flame to dim?
3. **How does a Guardian address each Wound specifically?** What actions contribute to healing each one?
4. **Is the Wound of Belonging the one without a visible metric?** (Consistent with Belonging having no Harmony Index measure — but worth confirming explicitly.)
5. **Can a Wound be fully healed, or only reduced?** This affects the long-term narrative.

### Writing brief

The Five Wounds entry must produce:
- [ ] Overview (what the Five Wounds are and how they differ from physical damage)
- [ ] Relationship to Pillar imbalance (one paragraph)
- [ ] Individual entry for each Wound containing:
  - Official name (finalized)
  - Which Pillar it corresponds to
  - What was lost / how civilization was affected
  - Physical or world manifestation (if any)
  - How it feels in daily life during the Restoration Era
  - How Guardians can address it
  - What recovery looks like
- [ ] Severity note (why no ranking exists / why Belonging is considered deepest)

## 4. CRYSTAL NETWORK

### What is confirmed and safe to build on

**Purpose and function (fully locked)**
- World-spanning knowledge connection system
- Connected Archives, researchers, discoveries, historical records, cultural records, companion histories, Realm knowledge
- Physical form: crystalline structures throughout Realms
- Function: distributed information, preserved discoveries, protected knowledge, reduced isolation
- Spiritual meaning: civilization's commitment to preserving truth
- Archive preserved; Crystal Network distributed — complementary relationship

**Current state (locked)**
- Partially dormant; some nodes survive; many pathways broken
- Companions interacted with Network sites; some companion memories associated with Crystal locations
- Guardians restore connections as core gameplay

### Key questions to answer before writing

1. **Who built the Crystal Network?** Was it a collective project? A single House (Lumina seems most likely)? Ancient Harmonists? This affects its origin story and the emotional weight of its loss.
2. **How were nodes created?** What was the process — physical construction, ritual, both?
3. **How does knowledge move through it?** Is it like a neural network — passive and ambient? Or does it require active use — someone must place a Memory Crystal to contribute?
4. **What does restoration look like in practice?** When a Guardian restores a node, what happens visually and narratively?
5. **Was any part of the Network intentionally hidden or protected before the Shattering?** (This could tie to open mysteries.)
6. **Relationship to the Memory Ocean?** The Memory Ocean is connected to memory — does it have any relationship to the Crystal Network?

### Writing brief

The Crystal Network entry must produce:
- [ ] Overview (what it is, what it did, what it symbolized)
- [ ] Origin account (who built it, when, why)
- [ ] How it functioned (technical and experiential description)
- [ ] Relationship to the Archive (how they worked together)
- [ ] Relationship to Companions (how companions interacted with it)
- [ ] What happened during the Shattering (how and why it failed)
- [ ] Current state in the Restoration Era (what survives, what is broken)
- [ ] What restoration looks and feels like
- [ ] Notable surviving nodes (at least 2–3 named examples, one per Realm type)

## 5. LANTERN NETWORK

### What is confirmed and safe to build on

**Purpose and function (locked)**
- Connected people, not information — the human complement to the Crystal Network
- Communities maintained Lantern sites; travelers followed Lantern routes; gatherings formed around them
- Lantern Keepers: maintain sites, preserve routes, welcome travelers, carry stories, protect traditions
- The Great Archive is the Lantern Network's successor in the Restoration Era
- Crystal Network = memory; Lantern Network = belonging — together the two foundations of civilization

**Symbolic meaning (locked)**
- Lantern = symbol of hope, belonging, guidance, connection
- *"A lantern is not merely a light. It is a promise that no traveler walks alone."*
- Common practices: lit during festivals, guide travelers, commemorate remembrance

**Current state (locked)**
- Some Lanterns still burn; some routes active; some Keepers remain; many communities seek restoration

**Open mystery (locked)**
- The Seventh Lantern — referenced in ancient records, nature and location unknown

### Key questions to answer before writing

1. **Were Lantern Keepers a formal order or informal tradition?** Did they have names, titles, initiation, succession?
2. **What did a Lantern site look like?** Architecture, scale, visual identity?
3. **How did the Lantern Network work in practice?** Was there a keeper at every site, or did communities maintain them collectively?
4. **What is the Seventh Lantern?** This is an open mystery — but we need enough of a sketch to write around it without accidentally closing it.
5. **How does the Archive succeed the Lantern Network?** Does the Archive literally take over Lantern routes? Or is it a spiritual/philosophical succession?
6. **Were there Lantern-specific traditions that predated the Lantern Network?** (Older folk practices the Network formalized?)

### Writing brief

The Lantern Network entry must produce:
- [ ] Overview (what it was, what it represented, how it differed from the Crystal Network)
- [ ] Origin account (how it began — more organic than the Crystal Network?)
- [ ] How it functioned (routes, sites, the role of Keepers)
- [ ] Lantern Keeper section (who they were, how they trained, what they believed)
- [ ] Major Lantern traditions and rituals (at least 3–4 specific practices)
- [ ] What happened during the Shattering (how and why routes fragmented)
- [ ] Current state in the Restoration Era (what survives)
- [ ] The Archive succession — how and why the Archive became the Lantern Network's heir
- [ ] The Seventh Lantern — teaser entry (what is known, what is mystery)

## 6. RESTORATION ERA

### What is confirmed and safe to build on

**World state (locked)**
- Post-fragmentation, not post-apocalyptic — hope survived
- Civilization no longer fully remembers itself but is actively working to
- What survived: Houses, Archive, some companions, some Lantern routes, some Crystal nodes, many communities, hope
- What remains broken: lost histories, broken connections, missing records, unanswered questions, forgotten traditions

**Emotional tone (locked)**
Hopeful, reflective, optimistic, curious, restorative

**Guardian role (locked)**
- Guardians arrive during this era; purpose is contribution, not conquest
- They help restore Memory, Community, Discovery, Harmony, Legacy, Belonging

**Five Wounds are ongoing (locked)**
- Multiple Wounds continue during this era — it is not healed, it is healing

### Key questions to answer before writing

1. **How long after the Shattering does the game begin?** Decades? Centuries? The answer affects how much was lost and how much institutional memory survives.
2. **Who leads civilization right now?** The Houses? A rebuilt council? Is there no central leadership?
3. **What is the state of the Harmony Council?** Did it survive? Was it destroyed? Has it been reformed?
4. **How does a new Guardian first arrive?** What is the moment of entry? Where do they appear? Who greets them?
5. **What does the Arena Realm look and feel like at the start of the game?** What is visible, what is broken, what gives hope?
6. **What does a citizen's daily life look like now, compared to the First Harmony?** What has been lost from daily experience?

### Writing brief

The Restoration Era entry must produce:
- [ ] World state overview (how civilization feels at the start of the game)
- [ ] What survived — in detail (not a bullet list — narrative description)
- [ ] What was lost — in detail
- [ ] Governance in the Restoration Era (who leads, how decisions are made)
- [ ] The Houses in the current era (how each House operates now vs. First Harmony)
- [ ] Daily life now vs. the First Harmony (contrast entry)
- [ ] Guardian entry point narrative (how a Guardian first experiences this world)
- [ ] Current state of the Five Wounds in this era
- [ ] The emotional arc available to a Guardian (from arrival to legacy)

## OPEN QUESTIONS — HIGHEST PRIORITY

These are the questions I believe must be answered before Tier 1 production writing begins. They are ranked by impact — a wrong answer to a top question would require rewrites across multiple entries.

| Priority | Question | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Who wrote the Book of Harmony?** | Determines voice, authority, and era-appropriateness of the text |
| 2 | **How long did the First Harmony last?** | Affects the weight of its loss and what "forgetting" means |
| 3 | **Are the Five Wounds names confirmed?** | Finalizing names affects all future worldbuilding language |
| 4 | **What triggered the Pillar drift?** | Without a proximate cause (even a non-villain one), the Shattering feels arbitrary |
| 5 | **Who leads civilization during the Restoration Era?** | Affects tone, Guardian entry point, and player's first relationships |
| 6 | **What is Kael's reframed role?** (Conflict 3) | Arena Champion framing must be resolved before writing Kael into any narrative or companion guide content |
| 7 | **What is the status of Lumi, Ember, Frosty?** (Conflict 6) | Affects companion planning for Tier 3 |
| 8 | **How does a Guardian first arrive?** | This is the emotional entry point of the entire franchise |
