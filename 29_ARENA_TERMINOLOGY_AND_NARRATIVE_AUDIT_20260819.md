# Arena First-Slice Terminology and Narrative Audit

**Version:** 1.0  
**Date:** 19 August 2026 (AEST)  
**Status:** Decision-safe audit; no canon changes made  
**Scope:** Arena first-slice documentation available in the archive and active governance records.

## Authority Method

This audit applies the project authority order: current creator direction, then active `DECISION_LOG.md`, then locked canon and active governance records, followed by archive drafts. When active records conflict internally, the conflict is preserved rather than silently resolved. Arena-specific prototype documents are treated as **draft creator-review materials**, not locked canon.

## Approved, Safe, and Flagged Language

| Category | Use / retain | Avoid / flag | Reason |
|---|---|---|---|
| Civic framing | gathering, crossroads, square, paths, records, care, welcome, contribution, making room | battleground, tournament, contender, opponent, arena fight | Arena is locked as civic assembly, not combat space. |
| Puzzle framing | restore, remember, notice, align, reconnect, assemble, reveal, guide, attune | beat, clear, defeat, destroy, blast, crush, dominate | Puzzles restore meaningful fragments of civilization. |
| Success framing | recognition, return, reconnection, the square remembers, a small thing restored | victory, winner, triumph, conquest, high score, perfect clear | Success must not be dominance/power feedback. |
| Retry framing | not yet, incomplete, take another look, notice, still there | fail, loss, punishment, out of lives, try harder | Player well-being and gentle retry rule. |
| UI CTAs | Look around, Show me, Begin restoring, Try again, Return to the square, Read fragment, Continue, End for now | Play level, Battle, Fight, Power up, Boost, Clear board, Victory, Next level, Out of energy | Draft UI pack aligns with locked tone exclusions. |

| Dusk/Twilight | Dusk (mechanical/UI); Twilight (narrative/realm) if actually relevant | Dark gem, darkness spreading, corrupting darkness | Locked terminology/tone decision. |

## Findings

The available Arena slice material is strongly aligned with the locked civic/restorative baseline in its current draft form. Its main risk is not overt combat copy but **status drift**: draft prototype text is repeatedly presented as recommended/default, while the root first-experience specification and exact implementation are unavailable. The most conservative implementation approach is to externalize the draft copy, label it `Draft — creator review`, and require a source-path and decision link before marking it as active content.

## Deliberately Unchanged

This audit does not rewrite dialogue, rename projects, approve assets, choose the puzzle mechanic, settle the Heart of the Core conflict, modify Harmony Flow, or change the active decision/canon/priority records.
