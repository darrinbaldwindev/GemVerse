## Festival Design Principles (Canon-Locked)

| Principle | Application |
|-----------|-------------|
| Restoration, not celebration | Each festival repairs a specific Wound aspect |
| Communal participation | Citizens, companions, Guardians co-create |
| No spectators | Everyone contributes; no "audience" role |
| Seasonal/Threshold alignment | Tied to Lantern cycles, Realm seasons, Pillar rhythms |
| Archive Record creation | Each festival generates a Festival Record (puzzle reward) |
| Companion leadership | Specific companions co-host per festival |
| Citizen Path progression | Festival participation advances citizen Paths |

## 1. Bloom Equinox (Crystal Forest)

**Canon Anchors**
- Realm: Crystal Forest
- Wound: Knowledge — Fragmentation (primary); Growth — Decay (secondary)
- Pillar: Knowledge
- Season/Trigger: Crystal Network resonance peak (spring equinox)

**In-World Origin**
Created by Lumina scholars and Verdance Stewards jointly during First Harmony Year 120. The Crystal Forest nodes required periodic "resonance flowering" — synchronized calibration where all nodes bloomed with light simultaneously. The festival combined this technical necessity with Verdance growth celebrations. When the Shattering began, the Bloom Equinox was the first festival to go silent. Its restoration marks the Crystal Forest's return to the network.

**Festival Structure**

**Phase 1: Preparation (Days 1-3) — "Root & Resonance"**
- **Community Pattern:** Citizens plant "resonance seeds" in a spiral pattern matching the Realm's node map (127 seeds = 127 nodes)
- **Citizen Path Activities:**
  - Scholars (Elara): Calibrate node resonance sequences (Crystal Resonance puzzles)
  - Mentors (Rowan): Teach seed-planting technique
  - Harmonists (Solen): Assess Realm Pillar balance pre-festival
  - Pathfinders (Taren, Lyra): Verify Lantern-lit routes from other Realms
- **Companion Rituals:** Verdant tends the central "Mother Node"; Prism reads resonance patterns aloud

**Phase 2: Convergence (Day 4) — "Full Bloom"**
- **Cross-Realm Arrivals:** Lantern-lit paths converge at Crystal Forest Heart
- **Central Ritual:** Simultaneous resonance calibration — all 127 nodes bloom in sequence. Each node's bloom confirms: "I am here. I am connected. I remember."
- **Archive Record:** Festival Record — Bloom Equinox [Year] documents: Realms attended, nodes bloomed, which failed, knowledge fragments flowing

**Phase 3: Integration (Days 5-7) — "Seed & Share"**
- **Harmony Alignment:** Knowledge/Growth rebalancing using bloom data
- **Legacy Seal:** First Calibration Seal (if Knowledge threshold met)
- **Departure:** Visitors carry "resonance cuttings" — crystals that grow into Lantern beacons

**Companion Roles**
- **Primary: Verdant** — Tends Mother Node; guides seed-planting; shares growth-memory fragments
- **Supporting: Prism** — Reads resonance patterns; identifies knowledge fragments
- **Visiting: Nimbus, Galewing** — Scout routes; carry resonance cuttings

**Citizen Path Advancement**
| Path | Activity | Advancement |
|------|----------|-------------|
| Scholar (Elara) | Transcribe resonance patterns → Archive | Knowledge Fragment credit |
| Mentor (Rowan) | Teach seed-planting to 3+ newcomers | Stewardship recognition |
| Harmonist (Solen) | Lead Knowledge/Growth rebalancing | Pillar balance credit |
| Pathfinder (Taren, Lyra) | Verify 2+ cross-Realm routes | Route restoration credit |

**Puzzle Integration**
| Puzzle Type | Festival Role | Archive Reveal |
|-------------|---------------|----------------|
| Crystal Resonance | Node calibration | Knowledge Fragment |
| Community Pattern | Resonance seed spiral | Festival Record — Bloom Equinox |
| Harmony Alignment | Knowledge/Growth rebalancing | Harmonist Balance Record |
| Legacy Seal | First Calibration Seal | Sealed Lumina Founder Testimony |

**Harmony Index Impact**
- Belonging Index: +15 per participant
- Realm Harmony (Crystal Forest): +25
- Cross-Realm Connection: +10 per visiting Realm
- Wound Healing: Knowledge Fragmentation -5%, Growth Decay -3%

**Visual Framing**
- Palette: Crystal Blue + Emerald Green + Lantern Gold
- Lantern behavior: Pulse in resonance sequence

- Wound: Growth — Decay (primary); Resilience — Community (secondary)

**Phase 2: Convergence (Day 4) — "Shared Warmth"**

- **Archive Record:** Festival Record — Ember Warmth [Year] documents: lineages presented, vent stability, new protocols, apprentice graduations

**Phase 3: Integration (Days 5-7) — "Spore & Story"**

## 4. Lantern Gathering (Arena Realm — Harmony Plaza)

**Canon Anchors**
- Realm: Arena Realm
- Wound: Community — Division (primary); All Wounds (secondary)
- Pillar: Belonging
- Season/Trigger: Lantern Network sync (quarterly)

**In-World Origin**
First Lantern Gathering was spontaneous in First Harmony Year 210. A Pathfinder returned to find their home Lantern unlit. Citizens gathered, relit it together, shared a meal. Became the "quarterly check-in" for civilization. Arena's Harmony Plaza built around this gathering. During the Threshold, Lanterns failed one by one; gatherings shrank; Plaza went silent.

**Festival Structure**

**Phase 1: Preparation (Days 1-3) — "Path & Plaza"**
- **Community Pattern:** Citizens weave "connection tapestries" — threads representing cross-Realm relationships
- **Citizen Path Activities:**
  - All Paths: Report restoration progress (Harmony Alignment)
  - Mentors (Rowan): Welcome newcomers; orient to Plaza
  - Scholars (Elara): Compile cross-Realm reports
  - Harmonists (Solen): Assess civilization-wide Pillar balance
  - Pathfinders (Taren, Lyra): Verify Lantern routes; guide arrivals
- **Companion Rituals:** Spark lights central Lantern; Galewing arrives with route reports

**Phase 2: Convergence (Day 4) — "Nine Flames"**
- **Central Ritual:** Simultaneous Lantern lighting — all 9 Realm Lanterns ignite in sequence as their Realm's citizens arrive. Central Lantern (Arena) pulses last, connecting all. Each Lantern's color reports its Realm's Harmony Index.
- **Archive Record:** Festival Record — Lantern Gathering [Quarter/Year] documents: Realms present, Lantern statuses, restoration progress, new connections

**Phase 3: Integration (Days 5-7) — "Thread & Return"**
- **Harmony Alignment:** Civilization-wide 7-Pillar rebalancing
- **Legacy Seal:** First Gathering Seal (if Belonging threshold met)
- **Departure:** Citizens carry "connection threads" — woven fibers becoming Lantern beacons

## 7. Convergence Celebration (Celestial Nexus)

- Wound: Cross-Wound — Harmony (primary); All Wounds (secondary)
- Pillar: Harmony
- Season/Trigger: Pillar alignment (rare — "Great Convergence" every 7 years)

- **Central Ritual:** Simultaneous Pillar resonance — all 7 Pillars' Light-beams converge at Nexus center, forming single column of white light. Representatives add Pillar stones to column. Column pulses with civilization's combined Harmony Index.
- **Archive Record:** Festival Record — Convergence Celebration [Cycle/Year] documents: Pillar readings, all 9 Realm reports, Wound metrics, recalibration actions

**Phase 3: Integration (Days 8-14) — "Action & Return"**

## 10. Harmony Week (All Realms — Cross-Realm)

**Canon Anchors**
- Realm: All 9 Realms simultaneously
- Wound: All Five Wounds
- Pillar: All Seven Pillars
- Season/Trigger: Restoration Era anniversary (annual)

**In-World Origin**
Harmony Week began Restoration Era Year 1 — first anniversary of first Guardian relighting a Lantern. Declared "the week we remember we are one civilization." Each Realm hosts its own week-long festival simultaneously, connected by Lantern Network. Ends with civilization-wide Harmony Alignment. During early Restoration, Harmony Week was fragile — few Realms participated, Lanterns flickered. Now it's the civilization's heartbeat.

**Festival Structure**

**Phase 1: Preparation (Days 1-3) — "Route & Realm"**
- **Community Pattern:** Each Realm weaves "Harmony banner" — 7 panels (one per Pillar) showing Realm's Pillar state. Banners connect via Lantern-lit routes forming civilization-wide tapestry.
- **Citizen Path Activities (All Realms):**
  - All Paths: Prepare Realm reports; welcome travelers
  - Pathfinders: Verify all 9 Realm routes active
  - Harmonists: Pre-assess Pillar states
  - Scholars: Compile anniversary Archive
- **Companion Circulation:** Each Companion spends one day in each Realm (9 days, 9 Realms) via Lantern Network "blink"

**Phase 2: Convergence (Days 4-7) — "Seven Days, Nine Realms, One Civilization"**
- **Daily Focus:**
  - Day 4: Knowledge (Crystal Forest hosts)
  - Day 5: Discovery (Sky Citadel + Twilight Frontier host)
  - Day 6: Growth (Ember Depths + Crystal Forest host)
  - Day 7: Memory (Frozen Expanse + Memory Ocean host)
  - *Resilience, Harmony, Belonging woven throughout*
- **Central Ritual (Day 7 Dusk):** Simultaneous Lantern lighting across all 9 Realms — "Civilization Pulse." Arena's central Lantern shows combined Index.
- **Archive Record:** Festival Record — Harmony Week [Year] documents: all 9 Realm reports, civilization Harmony Index, Wound metrics, Guardian commitments

**Phase 3: Integration (Post-Week) — "Commitment & Return"**
- **Harmony Alignment:** Civilization-wide 7-Pillar rebalancing (month-long)
- **Legacy Seal:** Annual Commitment Seal (if civilization Harmony threshold met)
- **Return:** Citizens carry "Harmony threads" — banner fibers becoming permanent Lantern beacons

**Companion Roles**
- **All 8 Launch Companions:** Circulate through all 9 Realms (one per day + Arena)
